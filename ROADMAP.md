# Roadmap

The goal: a slopless feature film from only a script. This is a direction, not a quality promise or delivery schedule.

## Near term

- Clean-install feedback across macOS, Linux and Windows/Docker.
- Better per-stage planning/image/video cost visibility and provider budget controls.
- More robust continuity checks for cast, props, dialogue and final assembly.
- Expanded end-to-end fixture coverage and backup/restore tooling.
- Independent security review and a production-ready non-Cloudflare storage adapter.
- Provider capability discovery, model access diagnostics and more flexible rendering options.

## Later

- More provider adapters without weakening approval and recovery guarantees.
- Collaborative studios with proper tenant isolation and roles.
- Optional managed hosting with reviewed provider commercial terms, prepaid wallets and transparent service pricing.
- A referral program at monetization: $100 per new referred member only after a completed video of at least five minutes.
- Optional commercial breaks in videos of at least five minutes, sponsorship and creator payouts. Revenue splits and ad frequency are not defined yet.

Monetization items are future requirements. This release does not activate them or promise a payout.
