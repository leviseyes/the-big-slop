# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, impersonate others or publish private information. Respect creators' rights and the policies of the services used by the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an available private GitHub channel; do not repeat private information in a public report. Enforcement considers context and severity, from a warning through removal for serious or repeated abuse.
